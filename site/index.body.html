packing congruent shapes · 2D and 3D · equal-budget study

Does the path a piece takes through shape space change which packings a search finds?

To pack n copies of a shape in the smallest container, most searches move rigid pieces. Hardening starts each piece as the disk inscribed in it and grows it into the real shape while the container is pushed inward. This site holds every run of a study that compares hardening with four other paths on seven families of shapes and containers, under the same budget and the same tuning effort.

Five paths

A piece's state is γ·[(1−τ)·P ⊕ τρ·D]: the polygon P scaled toward its incenter, plus a disk of radius τρ, all scaled by γ. Every method is a path through (γ, τ).
